How to read a card

  1. Rank and suit. The suit is the subject: Hearts for belonging, Diamonds for ambition, Clubs for streets, Spades for weather and time. The rank is the hour. Lay a suit out from 2 to 10 and you get one day, 6 AM to 11 PM.
  2. City and coordinates. Real coordinates run up the side of each half, so you always know which city you are standing in.
  3. The street, the time. Each half has a caption with the place and the hour. Both cities share the same local hour.
  4. The hidden line. One sentence runs across the seam in the middle. The first half reads from Bangalore's side.
  5. Turn it for New York. Turn the card around and the sentence finishes, now from New York's side. Every card works either way up.
